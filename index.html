What We Do

Precision Services,
Every Time

Every service is carried out with care, skill, and the attention to detail you deserve. No rush. No shortcuts.

Fades

Crisp, clean fades tailored to your head shape and style. From skin fades to taper fades — blended to perfection every time.

Straight-Razor Shaves

The full luxury experience — hot towel prep, precise straight-razor technique, and a finish that leaves your skin smooth and refreshed.

Eyebrow Shaping

Detail-focused brow grooming that frames your face and elevates your look. A small touch with a big impact.

Classic Haircuts

Traditional and modern cuts for all hair types. We listen, consult, and deliver exactly the style you're going for.

Kids' Cuts

Gentle, patient, and fun — we make sure the little ones feel comfortable and walk out looking sharp.

Beard Lineup

Sharp lines, defined edges, and a clean finish. Whether maintaining or reshaping, your beard gets the treatment it deserves.

Who We Are

Quality You Can Feel From the Moment You Sit Down

Gentlekutz is more than a barbershop — it's a commitment. From the first consultation to the final touch-up, every client gets a barber who's fully present, fully skilled, and fully invested in making you look and feel your best.

Serving the Stony Island corridor and all of Chicago, Gentlekutz has built a reputation for saving bad haircuts, delivering quality under pressure, and earning the kind of loyalty that only comes from consistently excellent work.
